The Two Repositories in Harmony

In v18, dom-primitive-engine has zero internal event binding code:

  • dom-primitive-bindings acts as the Trigger / Wiring Layer. It listens once on the container and catches bubbling events.
  • dom-primitive-engine acts as the Traversal / Extraction Layer. It receives the matched element and runs instructions.json to output clean JSON.
